## Governance relationship

Governed repositories remain sovereign execution systems.

Portfolio Governor reads their evidence and evaluates:

1. strategic alignment;
2. constitutional compliance/control gaps;
3. strategic debt;
4. capital efficiency;
5. cross-project lessons.

It does not write to project repositories.

## Review cadence

Run Governor review after a material milestone, approximately five significant experiments, an architecture reset, repeated negative evidence, substantial scope expansion, a material incident, or a meaningful change in economic trajectory.

Run allocation after governance when attention allocation may materially change.
