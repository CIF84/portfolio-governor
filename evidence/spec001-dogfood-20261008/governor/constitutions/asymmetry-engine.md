# Asymmetry Engine — Project Constitution

## Purpose
Systematically discover and exploit repeatable economic asymmetries capable of becoming low-maintenance income assets.

## Economic objective
Build a portfolio capable of producing at least **40,000 CZK/month** of low-maintenance income.

## Success condition
```text
discover monetizable opportunities
→ launch bounded offers/resolutions
→ observe transactions/value capture
→ identify profitable survivors
→ demonstrate recurrence
→ reduce Roman-hours per unit of recurring profit
```

## Non-goals
- general economic-decision research for its own sake;
- maximizing experiment count;
- maximizing operating-system sophistication;
- generic orchestration/control infrastructure before economics earn it;
- one-off high-touch consulting opportunities that cannot plausibly become low-maintenance assets.

## Current strategic hypothesis
A sufficiently constrained search space combined with rigorous opportunity discrimination, standardized resolution, standardized experimentation/distribution, and early monetization can create a repeatable passive-income production mechanism.

The opportunity topology/search scope remains subject to strategic review.

## Current strategic debt
- narrow repeatable monetizable opportunity topology;
- scalable experimentation;
- standardized FORGE/resolution;
- standardized distribution;
- monetization/checkout;
- transaction and value-capture evidence;
- recurrence;
- mature unit economics;
- Roman-hours/month economics.

## Economic invariants
Direct compute cost alone is insufficient: opportunity cost and mature human operating burden count.

Prefer selling a defensible unit of value over indirect demand experiments when making it purchasable is cheaper and more informative.

A production candidate needs a plausible path to repeatability, standardized capture, and low ongoing human involvement.

## Review triggers
- multiple experiments improve internal machinery without reducing distance to value capture;
- monetization repeatedly deferred;
- research recursively studies research/process;
- a new generic platform/control capability is proposed;
- opportunity classes require bespoke distribution/monetization each time;
- passive-income economics become implausible;
- evidence suggests the original commercial brief should change.
