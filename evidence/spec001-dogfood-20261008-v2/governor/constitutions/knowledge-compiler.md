# Knowledge Compiler — Project Constitution

## Purpose
Turn difficult source material into trustworthy, cognition-efficient representations that materially reduce the effort required to understand it.

## Economic objective
Become a profitable standalone consumer/prosumer knowledge product.

## Success condition
Users repeatedly choose and pay for Knowledge Compiler because its artifacts help them understand difficult material better or faster than credible alternatives.

## Non-goals
- general knowledge-management research for its own sake;
- maximizing semantic sophistication independent of learner value;
- visualization as an end in itself;
- exposing internal compiler machinery merely because it is interesting;
- indefinite architecture research after a defensible Minimum Sellable State exists.

## Current strategic hypothesis
Generative semantic synthesis/abstraction, bounded by conservative trust validation and compiled into cognition-efficient representations, can outperform ordinary source consumption and generic explanation for difficult material.

## Current strategic debt
- external learner evidence beyond the owner;
- explicit Minimum Sellable State;
- monetization surface;
- transaction and repeat-use evidence;
- production inference/unit economics;
- future privacy/IP/data-lifecycle controls if arbitrary user documents are processed commercially.

## Review triggers
- core architecture repeatedly fails;
- 5+ material experiments without reducing product/economic distance;
- new capabilities proposed while no genuine sellable-state blocker remains;
- external learner/monetization testing repeatedly deferred;
- evidence contradicts the core cognitive-value hypothesis;
- user-document handling materially changes privacy, IP, security, or retention exposure.

## Governor 001
Strategic alignment: **ALIGNED**.

Capital: **WATCH** — commercialization debt is accumulating, but current core-compiler work remains justified.
