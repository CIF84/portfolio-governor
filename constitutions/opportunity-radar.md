# Opportunity Radar — Project Constitution

## Purpose
Increase Roman's access to and selection of attractive employment opportunities while minimizing wasted attention.

## Economic objective
Maximize career option value and expected employment income.

## Success condition
```text
valuable opportunities discovered
→ worthwhile applications
→ interviews
→ late-stage processes
→ offers
→ improved compensation / option value
```

## Monetization
Direct product monetization is secondary and optional. Lack of SaaS revenue is not project failure.

## Non-goals
- maximizing vacancy volume;
- optimizing internal metrics without improving career decisions;
- application automation without explicit authority;
- SaaS commercialization at the expense of primary personal economic value;
- inventing lifecycle continuity or candidate evidence.

## Current strategic hypothesis
Trustworthy market observation, persistent lifecycle state, candidate-aware decision intelligence, conservative uncertainty handling, and selective semantic reasoning can improve employment opportunity discovery and prioritization.

## Current strategic debt
- trustworthy Operational Epoch 2;
- prospective ranking validation after recovery;
- application/interview/offer outcome evidence;
- attribution of career outcomes to Radar assistance;
- durable private candidate-intelligence lifecycle.

## Hard project invariants
- lost lifecycle intervals remain unknown;
- historical read-only state never silently becomes current state;
- real candidate intelligence remains private and outside tracked public Git;
- synthetic candidate fixtures are never represented as the real candidate;
- consequential private state requires verified recovery appropriate to its risk;
- APPLY/recommendation does not authorize external application action.

## Review triggers
- state loss or recovery failure;
- candidate/private-data boundary change;
- source portfolio materially changes;
- ranking improves without downstream career evidence;
- application automation is proposed;
- regulatory/privacy exposure materially changes.

## Governor 001
Strategic alignment: **ALIGNED**.

Safety: **STRONG / RECOVERED** — current Epoch-2 controls demonstrate the state-safety principle learned from the prior loss incident.
